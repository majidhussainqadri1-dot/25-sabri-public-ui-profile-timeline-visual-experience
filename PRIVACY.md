# Privacy Contract

File 25 is a public presentation layer and follows data minimization.

It does not own or expose:

- identity documents or credentials;
- full dates of birth or guardian records;
- patient health histories, appointments, prescriptions, or searches;
- private messages, calls, saves, reading history, or private analytics;
- raw contact information without authoritative visibility consent.

Founder and verified-doctor professional contacts may be displayed only through approved visibility contracts. Minor contact information is always denied by File 25 when the authoritative Membership Core marks the user as a minor.

The timeline contains only rebuildable pointers and lightweight public display metadata. Native modules remain the source of truth.
