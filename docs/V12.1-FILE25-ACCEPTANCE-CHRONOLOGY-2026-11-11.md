## Confirmed source-level gap
`Visual_Acceptance::validate_evidence()` previously checked each `recorded_at` for valid ISO syntax, calendar correctness and future timestamps. However it did not compare the **Founder sign-off timestamp** with the individual visual evidence timestamps or staging record. Therefore an otherwise well-formed manifest could claim a Founder approval dated **before** the screenshot, test or staging evidence it purported to approve. This is a chronological-consistency defect in the metadata validator; it does not prove that any real acceptance record was fraudulent.

## Minimal, owner-safe remedy
- After all existing group, sign-off and staging format/commit checks, validate that the Founder sign-off time is no earlier than each required evidence record and staging environment record (allow at most 300 seconds of recorded-clock skew).
- Fail with a path-specific error for the first affected surface or input record; do not silently rewrite evidence time, claim that approval occurred, or invent reviewer credentials.
- Keep existing privacy, exact-commit, artifact reference/size/SHA format checks. Preserve File24 as the authority for checking actual screenshot bytes, independent reviewers, real artifact hash integrity, audit provenance and deployment gates.
